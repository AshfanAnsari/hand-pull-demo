About this demo

Why a hand pulls the page.

grabbit. is a fictional sample kitchen created to test a tactile entrance motion: a single arm enters from the top edge of the viewport, grips the hero, and pulls the page up into scroll before letting go.

Every dish and price on this page is illustrative sample data. The scroll position and the hand's vertical offset share one GSAP progress driver so the fingers stay locked to the exact spot they grabbed until they clear the top edge.
